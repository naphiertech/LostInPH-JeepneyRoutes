# LostInPH Domain Glossary

Canonical terms for the Philippine public transportation discovery and navigation platform.

| Term | Definition | Notes / Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Jeepney Route Code** | Alphanumeric route identifier displayed on the vehicle's side body and front windshield (e.g., `17C`, `04L`, `13C`, `MNL-T01`, `CDO-R2`). | Do not call it "bus line" unless referring to a distinct bus service. |
| **Signboard Placard** | Printed or LED strip placed behind the jeepney's front windshield listing key corridors and endpoints (e.g., `APAS • IT PARK • FUENTE • CARBON`). | Commuters rely on signboard placards as much as route codes. |
| **Terminal / Turning Point** | Official start/end staging bay or turnaround point where passengers line up and jeepneys wait to fill or dispatch. | Distinguish from informal roadside stops. |
| **Landmark Stop** | Recognizable civic, commercial, or street-corner reference point where commuters request boarding or alighting (e.g., `Opposite Robinsons Fuente`, `USC Talamban Gate 3`). | Jeepneys often stop at landmarks rather than numbered poles. |
| **Transfer Hub** | Shared intersection, terminal, or mall bay where passengers alight from one jeepney route and walk briefly to board a connecting route. | Always state the walking direction and landmark. |
| **Para Po Zone** | Approaching drop-off radius (one stop or ~300 m before destination) where LostInPH alerts the passenger to call out to the driver. | Use local phrasing (`Lugar lang!` in Cebuano/Bisaya, `Para po!` in Tagalog). |
| **Vehicle Type** | Fleet classification: `traditional_jeepney` (open-air / classic jeepney) or `modern_puv` (air-conditioned Class 2/3 modern PUV / e-jeep). | Fare minimums differ between traditional and modern PUVs. |
| **Verification Tier** | Provenance of route data: `ltfrb_reference` (anchored to official franchise corridors), `community_verified` (confirmed by local mappers), or `field_draft` (pending verification, clearly labeled unverified). | Never present `field_draft` routes as verified. |
| **Transit Operator** | Platform operations role (`Admin`) responsible for maintaining route registries, fare matrices, terminal coordinates, and triaging field reports. | _Avoid_ calling this persona a commuter or general end-user. |
| **Commuter Account** | Passenger role (`App`) discovering routes, planning multi-leg jeepney journeys, checking statutory fares, and tracking trips on mobile/web. | Isolated from administrative editing workflows. |
| **Fare Matrix** | Regulatory fare schedule defining the initial 4 km base fare and subsequent per-kilometer rate per vehicle class, plus mandatory 20% discounts (student, senior citizen, PWD) rounded to ₱0.25. | Always pair base and per-km rates with the applicable vehicle type. |
| **Route Advisory / Detour** | Operational notice announcing temporary loading-bay relocations, road closures, flooding bypasses, or peak congestion along a jeepney corridor. | Distinguish transient detours from permanent signboard changes. |
| **Field Report** | Passenger- or mapper-submitted notice reporting a corridor detour, fare discrepancy, stop move, or new landmark for operator triage (`needs-triage` -> `ready-for-human` / `ready-for-agent` / `wontfix`). | Triggered from the Commuter `"Report Issue"` action and reviewed in Admin Field Reports & Triage. |
| **Commuter Profile** | Passenger hub (`Profile` tab and top-right avatar entry) consolidating commuter identity, Home City preview map, weekly trip activity, statutory Fare Discount Pass selector, saved corridors and places, corridor advisories, theme settings, and Sign Out. | Keeps account/session actions out of the primary discovery top bar. |
| **Vehicle Route Artwork** | Layered vector illustration of a Traditional Jeepney (with front roof signboard) or Modern PUV (with LED destination strip) displaying the live route code and paired with a low-contrast destination landmark silhouette. | Dynamically parameterized per route so Admin-added corridors render consistent artwork automatically. |
| **Motion Token Scale** | Shared CSS custom properties (`--duration-*`, `--ease-*`, `--distance-*`, `--scale-*`, `--blur-*`) in `src/index.css` powering automatic tactile press feedback, focus rings, panel/modal reveals, error shakes, and reduced-motion accessibility across all current and future UI controls. | Animate only `transform`, `scale`, and `opacity`; never use `transition: all` or `transition-[height]`. |
| **Map Pinpoint** | User-dropped coordinate marker on the interactive map that splits the route list into **Recommended near pinpoint** (routes passing within walking radius, ordered by walking distance to the nearest stop) on top and **Other city routes** below. | Supports 1-tap promotion to Trip Planner Origin (`Board here`), Destination (`Go here`), or clear. |
| **Road-Snapped Corridor** | High-density street-following polyline traced along actual OpenStreetMap road networks (both for full routes and sliced Trip Planner legs) rather than straight stop-to-stop zigzags. | Admin-created routes also snap to road geometry via OSRM with a street-interpolated fallback. |
| **Store License Purchase** | One-time `₱199` paid download of the LostInPH mobile application via **Google Play Store** (Android) or **Apple App Store** (iOS), granting full offline/online multi-city route discovery and trip navigation. | Tracked alongside `operator_grant` accounts in the Admin Console. |
| **Net Operator Payout** | Take-home PHP revenue from `₱199` store purchases after deducting platform store commission (`15%` under Google Play / Apple Small Business Program or `30%` standard tier) and refunds. | Audited in the **Sales & Store Analytics** sub-module. |
| **LostInPH Brand Mark** | Custom geometric SVG emblem combining an **L-corridor road loop**, a **45° directional wayfinding compass pin**, and a **Philippine Jeepney roof signboard crown** in Mobility Green (`#5D9B19`) and Signal Amber (`#EA580C`). | Used consistently as the browser favicon (`favicon.svg`), Login Gateway mark, Admin Console mark, and Commuter App header icon. |
